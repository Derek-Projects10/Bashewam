About Bashewam School

Bashewam School is a leading educational institution committed to providing quality education that nurtures academic excellence, innovation, and strong moral values.

Established with the vision of developing well-rounded students, we offer a comprehensive curriculum from Grade 1 to Grade 12 with modern facilities and dedicated teaching staff.

Our mission is to prepare students to become responsible citizens and future leaders who can contribute positively to society.
